Introduction

I focus on LLM applications, self-evolving agents, and post-training. My work explores how capable agents can use tools, learn from experience, and remain simple enough to understand and improve.

LLM Applications

Building practical systems that turn language models into useful tools for real tasks.

Self-Evolving Agents

Exploring agents that synthesize tools, correct trajectories, and improve through interaction.

Post-Training

Studying training and feedback methods that make agent behavior more capable and reliable.
